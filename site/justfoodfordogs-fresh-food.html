JustFoodForDogs review, cost, quote and discount — FAQ

How much does JustFoodForDogs cost?

It publishes shelf prices rather than a quiz price: Chicken & Rice 14-pack $153.99, Turkey & Whole Wheat Macaroni 14-pack $167.99 and Beef & Russet Potato 14-pack $181.99, each pack 18 oz. The 21-pack sizes are $230.99, $251.99 and $272.99.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/collections/all · checked 2026-09-26

How much is shipping?

Free on orders over $49 (figured after discounts, before tax), and first-time subscription orders are eligible for free shipping too. Below that threshold the flat fee is $19.99, and there is no delivery to PO Boxes, APOs or FPOs.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/faq · checked 2026-09-26

Is there a first-order discount?

The site banner advertised 50% off your first subscription order plus free shipping on the check date. We repeat it only because justfoodfordogs.com prints it - check the Terms link next to the banner for current conditions.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/ · checked 2026-09-26

How much food is in a box?

Four box sizes: Small 14 x 18-oz packages, Medium 21 x 18-oz, Large 28 x 18-oz, and Extra Large 8 x 72-oz.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/faq · checked 2026-09-26

How long does the food keep?

Up to 18 months (547 days) in the freezer for frozen packs; thawed non-fish meals keep sealed in the fridge for up to 7 days and 5 days once opened. Thawed food should not be refrozen.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/faq · checked 2026-09-26

Where is the food made?

Hand-crafted in open-to-the-public kitchens you can visit, plus the company's own kitchen in Irvine, California; the homepage lists 12 open kitchens.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/faq · checked 2026-09-26
