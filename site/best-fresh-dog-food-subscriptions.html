What the three brands actually publish

Strip the marketing back and each brand discloses a different layer of the bill. Ollie publishes units - $1.57 and $1.00 a meal on the pricing page, under four dollars and $8 a day in the FAQ - and never converts one into the other. The Farmer's Dog publishes a single floor, about $2 a day, plus free shipping and a 48-state footprint. JustFoodForDogs publishes shelf prices, the only ones you can multiply yourself: $153.99 to $181.99 per 14-pack, free shipping over $49, otherwise a flat $19.99.

The first-order offers differ in kind, not just size: Ollie's banner is dynamic (50% and 70% served the same evening), The Farmer's Dog's homepage strip is a flat 50% off, and JustFoodForDogs publishes written terms - 50% off the first subscription order up to $139 maximum, then 5% off every order after that. Only one of the three tells you in writing what happens to your wallet on order number two.

Fresh dog food subscriptions - FAQ

Which fresh dog food subscription publishes a price you can check before signing up?

JustFoodForDogs. Its Shop All page and product pages print box prices - $153.99, $167.99 and $181.99 for a 14-pack of 18-oz packs - so you can price an order without handing over your dog's details. Ollie prints only starting prices per meal and The Farmer's Dog prints only a starting price per day; both reveal your actual figure inside their builders.

How much does Ollie cost per day?

Ollie's own FAQ: prices vary with your dog's size and portion size, the food 'starts at less than four dollars per day for small dogs who are exclusively fed Ollie', and 'averages $8 per day'. Those are Ollie's published range, not a quote - your number comes from its plan builder.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/faqs/ · checked 2026-09-27

How much does The Farmer's Dog cost per day?

Its FAQ answer is 'Plans start at about $2/day', and it says delivery options mostly range from 1 week to 2 months of food and include free shipping. Your exact price depends on age, weight, activity, recipe choice and more, and appears after the questionnaire - so $2 a day is a floor, not your bill.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/faq · checked 2026-09-27

How much does JustFoodForDogs cost?

It publishes shelf prices rather than a quiz price: Chicken & Rice 18 oz (14 Pack) $153.99, Turkey & Whole Wheat Macaroni (14 Pack) $167.99 and Beef & Russet Potato (14 Pack) $181.99, with 21-pack sizes at $230.99, $251.99 and $272.99. No per-day figure is printed on the pages read, so a daily cost would be your own arithmetic.
