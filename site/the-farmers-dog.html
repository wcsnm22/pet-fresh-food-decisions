The Farmer's Dog review, cost, quote and discount — FAQ

How much does The Farmer's Dog cost?

Its own FAQ answers this: plans start at about $2/day, and the exact price depends on your dog's age, weight, activity and recipe choice. Delivery options mostly range from 1 week to 2 months of food and include free shipping.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/faq · checked 2026-09-26

Is shipping really free?

Yes. The FAQ says deliveries include free shipping, and the homepage repeats 'Free shipping with any plan'.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/faq · checked 2026-09-26

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes - the FAQ says you can pause, cancel or reactivate anytime from your Customer Account, and the homepage lists 'Cancel anytime' as a plan feature.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/faq · checked 2026-09-26

Where does The Farmer's Dog ship?

To the 48 contiguous states. Fresh food arrives in refrigerated boxes, and Instant Fresh is shelf-stable for pantry storage.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/faq · checked 2026-09-26

What recipes are available?

Human-grade recipes featuring beef, chicken, turkey and pork, in Fresh and Instant Fresh serving styles, with both grain-free and grain-inclusive options. The brand says they are complete and balanced to AAFCO standards.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/faq · checked 2026-09-26

What happens if my dog doesn't like it?

The Farmer's Dog says it can send additional recipes to try, or you can opt for a refund if you donate the food to a shelter.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/faq · checked 2026-09-26

Is there a first-order discount?

The site showed 'You've been gifted 50% off your first purchase!' on the check date. We report it only because the brand's own homepage prints it; check the live page for current terms.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/ · checked 2026-09-26
