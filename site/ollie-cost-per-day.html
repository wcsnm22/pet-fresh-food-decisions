How much does Ollie cost per day? Under $4 to start, $8 on average - Ollie's own FAQ, checked 2026-09-26

Quick answer: Ollie answers this question itself, in its own FAQ - food starts at less than four dollars per day for small dogs who are exclusively fed Ollie, and averages $8 per day. Ollie adds that prices vary based on the size of your dog and the size of their portions, so both numbers are its own statements, not a quote for your dog. The Meal Plans page publishes a different unit entirely: Full Fresh Meals from $1.57 a meal and the Half Fresh Plan from $1.00 a meal, both labelled 'Starting at', and no per-day figure next to them. What Ollie does print about the bill: your starter box is the first two weeks of food, your account is charged three days before the box ships (no edits after that), delivery is free per the page title, and cancellation happens on the website rather than in the app. Every figure below carries its official source and a check date of 2026-09-26.

Deliberately left out: any estimated monthly bill, any breed-by-breed price table (Ollie does not publish one), and any figure from a third-party review or coupon site.

What Ollie actually tells you about the bill

Strip the marketing out and Ollie's public pages disclose five money facts: two per-meal starting prices, two per-day statements, and a billing rule. The starter box is your first two weeks of food, your card is charged three days before the box ships, and after that charge the order is locked. Delivery is advertised free in the page title, and the plan is a subscription you cancel on the website rather than in the app.

Ollie cost per day - FAQ

How much does Ollie cost per day?

Ollie's own FAQ answer: its food starts at less than four dollars per day for small dogs who are exclusively fed Ollie, and averages $8 per day. It says prices will vary based on the size of your dog and the size of their portions, so treat these as Ollie's published range, not your quote.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/faqs/ · checked 2026-09-26

Will I pay $8 a day?

Not necessarily - $8 is the average Ollie prints, not a fixed price. Ollie states that prices vary with your dog's size and portion size, and the exact figure comes from its plan builder. The official pages do not publish a price list by dog weight.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/faqs/ · checked 2026-09-26

How much does Ollie cost per meal?

On the Meal Plans page: Full Fresh Meals start at $1.57 a meal and the Half Fresh Plan starts at $1.00 a meal. Both are labelled 'Starting at', and no per-day figure is printed next to them.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/meal-plans · checked 2026-09-26

What is Ollie's cheapest plan?

The Half Fresh Plan is the one Ollie labels 'budget friendly', starting at $1.00 a meal - it is described as a freshly made half-serving added to your dog's mealtime. What the full plan costs for your dog is not published; the plan builder prices it.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/meal-plans · checked 2026-09-26

How much is Ollie for a small dog?

The only small-dog figure Ollie publishes is in its FAQ: food starts at less than four dollars per day for small dogs who are exclusively fed Ollie. No price for a specific small breed is published on the official pages.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/faqs/ · checked 2026-09-26

When does Ollie charge me?

Three days before your package ships, tied to your box delivery frequency. Once the order charges, Ollie says it cannot be edited or altered - to move the date you use Deliveries in your account, or contact its Canine Care team.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/faqs/ · checked 2026-09-26

Is delivery free?

Ollie advertises it as free: the Meal Plans page title reads 'Dog Meal Plans | Free Delivery | Ollie'. No minimum order is printed on the official pages we read on 2026-09-26.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/meal-plans · checked 2026-09-26

Can I cancel Ollie?

Yes, but from the website account rather than the app: sign in, open My Plan > My Info > Subscriptions, click Cancel Subscription for each dog, then confirm after a short survey. Cancelling turns off auto-renewal and does not affect orders already in progress; Ollie also prints a cancellation line, +1 650-405-3420, 9am-8pm ET Monday-Friday and 10am-7pm ET Saturday.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/faqs/ · checked 2026-09-26

Is there a discount on the first box?

Ollie advertised one on 2026-09-26, but the figure moved during our check: the Meal Plans page HTML showed 50% off your first box at 19:06 (UTC+8) and the homepage showed 70% off plus a free hat and dog bandana at 19:21. Ollie's pages do not print the offer's full terms, so confirm the banner and terms at checkout.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/ · checked 2026-09-26

What is in the first box?

Ollie says the starter box contains the first two weeks of food plus a welcome kit - a handy scoop and a puptainer to store the food.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/faqs/ · checked 2026-09-26
