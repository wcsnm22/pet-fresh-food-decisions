Best raw dog food delivery 2026: We Feed Raw, Darwin's and Maev prices, shipping and terms read from their own sites, checked 2026-09-27

Quick answer: three raw dog food delivery services publish their prices on their own sites, and each one uses a different ruler. We Feed Raw sells by the week - its beef, chicken and turkey recipe pages print $8.89/pound with a $16.10 weekly list price beside a $9.66 weekly price, lamb and duck at $10.71 and freeze-dried at $13.21, with free cold shipping anywhere in the contiguous U.S. Darwin's sells by the package - every pack is 2.0 lb, priced from $15.00 (BioLogics Raw Chicken) to $26.00 (Natural Selections Premium Duck), minimum order 10 lbs, free shipping from 20 lbs, and its homepage offers 70% off the first box. Maev sells by the bag - 20 lbs at $179 on subscription, printed as $6.39 a day, or $206 one-time at $7.36 a day, with free shipping over $249. No service prints a conversion into another service's unit, so the three numbers below sit side by side and are never subtracted. How this page was checked

On 2026-09-27 (UTC+8) we fetched the raw menu pages in plain HTML and read each brand's own copy: We Feed Raw's subscription page plus its beef, chicken, turkey, lamb, duck and freeze-dried recipe pages (prices taken from each page's own product data), Darwin's homepage, both beef product pages, its Shop All collection and its full FAQ, and Maev's raw food product page and shipping policy. Every number in the table below was on one of those pages on that date.

The finding worth keeping: these three services do not sell in the same unit, and each publishes a different shipping rule - free with no threshold, free at 20 lbs, free over $249. That combination, not a badge, is what decides which service is cheapest for your dog.

Deliberately excluded: every third-party ranking and taste test, any price from a retailer or marketplace, any monthly total built by multiplying a starting price by a feeding amount the brands do not print, and any conversion between the three units.

What the answer comes down to

Buy by the week if you want the smallest printed commitment. We Feed Raw's recipe pages put a weekly figure in front of you before you answer any question - $9.66 against a $16.10 list price for the three cheapest frozen recipes - and it is the only one of the three that prints free shipping with no threshold at all.

Buy by the pack if you want a shelf price with no builder. Darwin's lists every dog pack and its price on one collection page, from $15.00 to $26.00 per 2.0 lb, tells you the minimum order is 10 lbs and the free-shipping line is 20 lbs, and puts 70% off the first box on the homepage. It is also the only one of the three that publishes a feeding percentage and a day-by-day transition schedule.

Buy by the bag if you want a per-day number. Maev is the only service here that prints a daily figure itself - $6.39 a day on subscription for 20 lbs - and it is the only one that puts its free-shipping threshold in dollars ($249) rather than pounds. Read its shipping policy next to the 'pause or cancel anytime' line: the policy closes cancellations at order placement.

What none of them publishes: a conversion between units, a shipping fee amount for Darwin's orders under 20 lbs, a minimum order for We Feed Raw or Maev, or a day-by-day transition schedule from We Feed Raw. Those cells say so instead of guessing.

Best raw dog food delivery - FAQ

What is the best raw dog food delivery service?

It depends on which unit you want to shop in. We Feed Raw publishes weekly plan prices from $9.66 (list $16.10) at $8.89 a pound with free cold shipping in the contiguous U.S.; Darwin's publishes 2.0-lb packs from $15.00 with a 10-lb minimum order, free shipping from 20 lbs and 70% off a first box; Maev publishes a 20-lb bag at $179 on subscription ($6.39 a day) with free shipping over $249. None of the three prints a conversion into another's unit, so pick the service whose unit and shipping terms fit your dog, then read the price on that brand's own page.

How much does We Feed Raw cost?

Its recipe pages publish plan starting prices: $8.89 a pound with $16.10 shown as the weekly list price beside $9.66 a week for beef, chicken and turkey; $9.89 a pound with $17.85 beside $10.71 for lamb and duck; and $22.02 beside $13.21 a week for the freeze-dried beef and chicken recipes. Shipping is free in the contiguous U.S., and the FAQ says subscribing gets 10% off the first order.

How much does Darwin's raw dog food cost?

Every pack is 2.0 lb and prices are printed per pack: BioLogics Raw Chicken $15.00, BioLogics Raw Turkey $16.50, BioLogics Raw Beef $19.00, Natural Selections Premium Chicken $18.75, Beef $22.50, Turkey $20.75 and Duck $26.00. The minimum order is 10 lbs, shipping is free from 20 lbs in the contiguous 48 states, subscribers get 10% off every order, and the homepage offers 70% off the first box.

Source: https://www.darwinspet.com/collections/shop-all · checked 2026-09-27

How much is Maev raw dog food?

The product page prices the selected 20-lb bag at $179 on Subscribe & Save - printed as $6.39 a day with 10% off and pause or cancel anytime - and $206 as a one-time purchase, printed as $7.36 a day. Bag sizes run 10 to 80 lbs, delivery runs every 2 to 10 weeks, and shipping is free over $249.

Source: https://www.meetmaev.com/products/raw-food · checked 2026-09-27

Is raw dog food delivery free to ship?

Only one of the three prints free shipping with no threshold: We Feed Raw says all products are cold-shipped free anywhere in the contiguous U.S. Darwin's is free at 20 lbs or more (orders under 20 lbs carry a fee it does not print, and Alaska and Hawaii are a flat $150). Maev is free over $249, with all shipments insured.

Source: https://www.darwinspet.com/pages/ask-darwins-faqs · checked 2026-09-27

Can you cancel a raw food subscription at any time?

Both We Feed Raw and Darwin's say yes in writing: We Feed Raw prints 'No commitment' on its subscription page, and Darwin's lets you modify, pause, skip or cancel any time through your account. Maev's product page also prints 'Pause or cancel anytime', but its shipping policy adds that an order cannot be cancelled or refunded once it is placed and food cannot be returned after it ships - so the cancellation window closes at order placement.

Source: https://www.meetmaev.com/shipping-return · checked 2026-09-27

Does raw dog food delivery arrive frozen, and how do you store it?

We Feed Raw ships frozen patties with dry ice and says unopened meals keep up to 6 months in the freezer (thaw about 12 hours in the fridge, or 15-20 minutes in cool water). Darwin's says meals must stay frozen until 12-24 hours before feeding, should never be microwaved, and a thawed meal should not be refrozen. Maev ships flash-frozen food to serve frozen, thawed or gently warmed, leaving a thawed portion 10-15 minutes at room temperature first.

Source: https://wefeedraw.com/raw-food-subscription · checked 2026-09-27

Is raw dog food delivery safe?

Each brand publishes its own handling steps, and these are the brands' statements rather than an independent test: We Feed Raw says its food is made in a USDA-certified facility under BRC requirements with a cold-pressure (HPP) process; Darwin's says processing includes an organic acid bath and regular third-party testing; Maev says it uses USDA-certified proteins from USDA-inspected facilities and states USDA and FDA certified SQF Level 3 plus a Clean Label Project Purity Award. Your vet can tell you whether a raw diet fits your dog.

Source: https://wefeedraw.com/raw-food-subscription · checked 2026-09-27

Do the three prices on this page compare directly?

No. We Feed Raw publishes a weekly plan price and a per-pound figure, Darwin's publishes a per-2.0-lb-pack price with a 10-lb minimum, and Maev publishes a per-20-lb-bag price and a per-day figure. None of the three prints a conversion into either of the other units, so subtracting them would invent a number that appears on no official page.

Source: https://www.meetmaev.com/products/raw-food · checked 2026-09-27

How do you switch a dog to raw food?

Darwin's prints the most specific schedule: 7-10 days for a normal transition and 14-21 days for picky eaters, seniors or sensitive stomachs, with 2-4% of body weight a day as the adult feeding starting point. Maev's product page advises using its transition guidance for the next 10-14 days. We Feed Raw publishes feeding guidelines on its recipe pages and includes them with the delivery, but the pages read on 2026-09-27 carry no day-by-day transition schedule.
