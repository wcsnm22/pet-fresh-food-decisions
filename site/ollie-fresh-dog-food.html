Ollie review, cost, quote and discount — FAQ

How much does Ollie cost?

Ollie publishes starting prices only: Full Fresh Meals from $1.57 a meal and the Half Fresh Plan from $1.00 a meal. The price for an individual dog is produced by Ollie's plan builder, so treat the published figures as the floor, not the final bill.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/meal-plans · checked 2026-09-26

What recipes does Ollie offer?

Five: Beef, Chicken, Lamb, Turkey and Pork. Ollie says you can choose a single recipe or mix and match them, and that meat is always the first ingredient with no added hormones or fillers.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/meal-plans · checked 2026-09-26

Is Ollie's food human-grade?

Ollie labels its Full Fresh Meals as made from 100% human-grade meat, fruits, veggies, and grains, and its homepage banner carries a 'Human Grade' value alongside 'Vet+Chef Developed'.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/meal-plans · checked 2026-09-26

Does Ollie have a first-order discount?

Yes, as published on the check date: ollie.com advertised a first-box discount plus a free hat and dog bandana. The banner figure was not stable that day - 50% off in the Meal Plans page HTML at 19:06 (UTC+8) and 70% off on the homepage at 19:21 - so treat it as dynamic and confirm the current terms on Ollie's site before ordering.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/ · checked 2026-09-26

How do I get a price for my own dog?

Use Ollie's plan builder (the Get Started flow on ollie.com), which asks about your dog and then shows the plan. The public pages only show the starting price per meal.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/meal-plans · checked 2026-09-26

How do I contact Ollie support?

Email caninecare@ollie.com, call or text 844-88-OLLIE (65543), or use live chat with canine support - all three are listed on Ollie's FAQ page.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/faqs · checked 2026-09-26
