FAQ

What does fresh dog food delivery cost per day?

Only two of the three brands publish a per-day or per-meal figure: Ollie lists meals from $1.57 (Full Fresh) and $1.00 (Half Fresh), and The Farmer's Dog says plans start at about $2/day. JustFoodForDogs does not quote a per-day price - it publishes box prices, from $153.99 for a 14-pack of Chicken & Rice.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/meal-plans · checked 2026-09-26

Which of these brands is a subscription?

All three sell on a subscription basis: The Farmer's Dog says you can pause, cancel or reactivate anytime, JustFoodForDogs' promo is tied to a first subscription order, and Ollie's FAQ walks customers through managing and cancelling a subscription.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/faq · checked 2026-09-26

Is shipping free?

The Farmer's Dog includes free shipping with any plan; JustFoodForDogs ships free over $49 (or on a first subscription order) and charges $19.99 below that; Ollie lists Free Shipping as a homepage value without printing a minimum.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/faq · checked 2026-09-26

Do any of them offer a first-order discount?

All three advertised one on the check date: Ollie 50% off the first box plus a free hat and bandana, The Farmer's Dog 50% off the first purchase, JustFoodForDogs 50% off the first subscription order plus free shipping. We only repeat offers the brand's own site publishes.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/ · checked 2026-09-26

How big are the packs and how long does the food keep?

JustFoodForDogs sells 18-oz packs in boxes of 14, 21 or 28 (plus 8 x 72-oz XL) and says frozen food keeps up to 18 months. The Farmer's Dog delivers 1 week to 2 months of food at a time and says Fresh packs should stay below 40°F; Instant Fresh is shelf-stable.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/faq · checked 2026-09-26

Where do these brands ship?

The Farmer's Dog ships to the 48 contiguous states. JustFoodForDogs cannot deliver to PO Boxes, APOs or FPOs. Ollie's public pages state free shipping but the check date did not show a destination list, so confirm at checkout.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/faq · checked 2026-09-26
