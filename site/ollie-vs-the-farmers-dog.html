Ollie vs The Farmer's Dog: what each brand publishes about price, checked 2026-09-26

Quick answer: the two brands do not publish the same kind of number, so a straight price-versus-price verdict does not exist on their own sites. The Farmer's Dog prints one figure - plans start at about $2/day, with delivery options mostly ranging from 1 week to 2 months of food and including free shipping. Ollie prints per-meal figures instead - Full Fresh Meals from $1.57 a meal, Half Fresh Plan from $1.00 a meal - and answers in per-day terms only inside its FAQ: food starts at less than four dollars a day for small dogs fed Ollie exclusively, and averages $8 a day. Neither brand prices your dog on a public page (Ollie uses its plan builder, The Farmer's Dog uses a questionnaire), so the numbers below are floors, not bills. The exclusive bit: each brand publishes the number the other one does not

This is the whole comparison in one line: only The Farmer's Dog publishes a per-day starting price, and only Ollie publishes per-meal starting prices. That is not a stylistic quirk - it changes what you can honestly calculate.

Ollie gives you $1.57 and $1.00 but no official meals-per-day figure, so the public pages never multiply them out; its per-day answer lives in a separate FAQ entry ($8 average, under $4 for small dogs). The Farmer's Dog gives you about $2 a day and never prints a per-meal number anywhere on its FAQ. So a reader who types 'Ollie vs The Farmer's Dog price' into a search box will find sites doing the multiplication for them; the brands do not.

The terms diverge in the same pattern. Ollie pins down the mechanics to the day (charged three days before shipping, freezer life of 6 months unopened, 4 days once thawed, cancel on the website but not the app) while The Farmer's Dog keeps it loose (pause, cancel, reactivate anytime; food below 40°F on arrival; delivery from 1 week to 2 months). Neither is wrong - they are just different disclosures, and both are printed in the sources linked beside each row.

Ollie vs The Farmer's Dog - FAQ

Which brand publishes a per-day starting price?

Only The Farmer's Dog. Its FAQ says 'Plans start at about $2/day'. Ollie publishes per-meal starting prices on its Meal Plans page ($1.57 Full Fresh, $1.00 Half Fresh) and gives per-day figures only inside its FAQ - under $4 a day for small dogs fed Ollie exclusively, averaging $8 a day.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/faq · checked 2026-09-26

Is Ollie cheaper than The Farmer's Dog?

Their own pages do not answer that, and neither do we. The two publish different units (per meal versus per day), each prices your dog individually through its own tool, and neither brand publishes a head-to-head figure for the same dog. Anyone quoting a single winner is doing arithmetic the brands themselves do not do.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/meal-plans · checked 2026-09-26

How much does Ollie cost per day?

Ollie's own FAQ says its food starts at less than four dollars per day for small dogs who are exclusively fed Ollie, and averages $8 per day. It also says prices vary based on the size of your dog and the size of their portions, so the average is its own figure, not a quote for your dog.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/faqs/ · checked 2026-09-26

How much does The Farmer's Dog cost per day?

Its FAQ says plans start at about $2/day, and that your exact plan and price depend on age, weight, activity and recipe choice. The exact pricing options appear after you fill out the questionnaire.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/faq · checked 2026-09-26

Do either of them charge for shipping?

Both advertise it as free. Ollie's Meal Plans page title reads 'Dog Meal Plans | Free Delivery | Ollie'; The Farmer's Dog says its delivery options include free shipping. Neither page prints a minimum order for it.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/meal-plans · checked 2026-09-26

Where do they ship?

Ollie ships to pups in the continental United States and says it cannot ship to Alaska or Hawaii at this time. The Farmer's Dog ships to the 48 contiguous states.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/faqs/ · checked 2026-09-26

Can you cancel whenever you want?

Both say yes, with different mechanics. The Farmer's Dog: pause, cancel or reactivate anytime from your Customer Account. Ollie: cancel from your account on its website (not the app), one dog at a time, or call +1 650-405-3420 - cancelling turns off auto-renewal and does not affect orders already in progress.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/faqs/ · checked 2026-09-26

How do you store the food when it arrives?

Ollie: freezer up to 6 months unopened, 24 hours to thaw in the fridge, then up to 4 days to serve. The Farmer's Dog: keep it refrigerated or frozen and only feed packs that arrive frozen or very cold, still below 40°F - its Instant Fresh line is shelf-stable and needs no refrigeration.

Source: https://www.thefarmersdog.com/faq · checked 2026-09-26

Is there a first-order discount?

On 2026-09-26 both brands advertised one: The Farmer's Dog showed '50% off your first purchase', and Ollie's pages showed 50% at 19:06 (UTC+8) and 70% later the same evening. Ollie's banner figure changed within minutes of our check, so confirm the current offer and its terms on the brand's site before ordering.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/meal-plans · checked 2026-09-26

What is in the first delivery?

Ollie's starter box is the first two weeks of food plus a welcome kit with a scoop and a puptainer. The Farmer's Dog's delivery options mostly range from 1 week to 2 months of food, with the amount customized to your dog.

Source: https://www.ollie.com/faqs/ · checked 2026-09-26
