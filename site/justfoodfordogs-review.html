JustFoodForDogs review 2026: published prices, discount terms, shipping and storage, checked 2026-09-27

Quick answer: JustFoodForDogs is the fresh dog food brand that prints its prices instead of quoting you. Its three Fresh Frozen recipes list $153.99 (Chicken & Rice), $167.99 (Turkey & Whole Wheat Macaroni) and $181.99 (Beef & Russet Potato) for a 14-pack of 18-oz packs, with 21-pack, 28-pack and 72-oz sizes on the same pages. The subscription option was displayed at 50% off on the check date - $77.00, $84.00 and $91.00 against compare-at prices of $153.99, $167.99 and $181.99 - and the official promotion page caps that first-order discount at $139, adds free shipping, then 5% off every later subscription order. Shipping is free over $49 after discounts and before tax, otherwise a flat $19.99. Every figure below carries its official source URL and a check date of 2026-09-27.

How this page was checked

On 2026-09-27 (UTC+8) we fetched the three Fresh Frozen recipe product pages and the Shop All collection page in raw HTML, and read the FAQ, the Offers, Coupons & Promotion Details page and the terms of service directly. Variant prices were taken from each page's own product data, not from a screenshot or a listing elsewhere.

The finding worth keeping: JustFoodForDogs is the only brand in this comparison that lets you price an order before handing over your dog's details - and it is also the only one that writes down what happens after the first order (5% off every later subscription order). The 50% headline has a written ceiling: $139.

Deliberately left out: any per-day or per-serving cost (the brand publishes no per-day figure), any price from a retailer or marketplace, any stock or delivery estimate, and any figure that does not appear on an official page linked below.

What the review comes down to

Strip the copy back and the brand discloses four things most competitors do not. Every box size is priced publicly - 14, 21 and 28 packs of 18 oz, plus the 8-pack of 72 oz, on the recipe's own page. The subscription discount is written down: 50% off the first subscription order capped at $139, free shipping with it, and 5% off every order after that. Shipping has a number attached - free over $49, $19.99 below it. Storage has numbers attached too - 18 months frozen, 7 days sealed after thawing, 5 days once opened.

The differences between recipes are narrower than the price gap suggests: the real switches are life stage (Chicken & Rice covers all life stages including large-breed growth, the other two are maintenance recipes and the Beef page warns against growing puppies) and calories per pack - 774, 882 and 792 kcal per 18 oz.

JustFoodForDogs review - FAQ

How much does JustFoodForDogs cost?

It publishes shelf prices rather than a quote: $153.99 for Chicken & Rice, $167.99 for Turkey & Whole Wheat Macaroni and $181.99 for Beef & Russet Potato, each for a 14-pack of 18-oz packs. Larger boxes cost more on the same pages - 21-packs at $230.99, $251.99 and $272.99, 28-packs at $307.99, $335.99 and $363.99, and 8-packs of 72 oz at $287.99, $311.99 and $335.99.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/collections/all · checked 2026-09-27

Is the JustFoodForDogs subscription really 50% off?

On the check date the subscription option on all three recipe pages showed 50% off the 14-pack - $77.00, $84.00 and $91.00 against compare-at prices of $153.99, $167.99 and $181.99 - and the site banner read '50% Off First Subscription Order + Free Shipping'. The official terms qualify it: 50% off your first subscription order up to a $139 maximum discount, one per household, continental U.S. only, subject to change without notice.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/offers-coupons-promotion-details · checked 2026-09-27

What discount do I get after the first JustFoodForDogs order?

5% off all subsequent subscription orders, stated on the same official promotion page as the first-order offer. It is the only written ongoing discount the brand publishes - the first order's 50% is capped at a $139 maximum discount.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/offers-coupons-promotion-details · checked 2026-09-27

How much is JustFoodForDogs shipping?

Free on orders over $49, calculated after discounts and before tax, and first-time subscription orders are eligible for free shipping too. Below that threshold the flat fee is $19.99, frozen and dry goods ship separately with separate charges, and there is no delivery to PO Boxes, APOs or FPOs.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/faq · checked 2026-09-27

Can you cancel a JustFoodForDogs subscription anytime?

Its terms say 'You may cancel your Autoship at any time' - with one condition: cancel before the Autoship renews, otherwise the next order is billed. You cancel from your account, and the payment processor charges your first order at placement and then at the frequency you chose until you cancel.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/policies/terms-of-service · checked 2026-09-27

How long does JustFoodForDogs food keep?

Up to 18 months (547 days) in the freezer for frozen packs. Once thawed, non-fish meals stay fresh sealed for up to 7 days and 5 days after opening; fish-based recipes keep 5 days sealed and 3 days once opened. The FAQ says to thaw in the refrigerator - about 24 hours for an 18-oz pack - and never to refreeze thawed food.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/faq · checked 2026-09-27

Is JustFoodForDogs suitable for puppies?

It depends on the recipe. The Chicken & Rice page says the recipe is formulated to meet the AAFCO Dog Nutrient Profiles for all life stages, including growth of large size dogs, and marks it perfect for puppies and adult dogs. The Turkey & Whole Wheat Macaroni and Beef & Russet Potato pages state their feeding trials substantiate nutrition for adult maintenance, and the Beef page says it is not intended for growing puppies.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/products/chicken-rice · checked 2026-09-27

Does JustFoodForDogs use preservatives?

Its FAQ answers plainly: 'Our only preservative is the freezer. We do not add any preservatives at all.' The product pages separately print 'No Additives, Preservatives, or Added Growth Hormones', and the FAQ says canine diets are balanced to NRC standards and then tested through AAFCO feeding trials.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/faq · checked 2026-09-27

Where is JustFoodForDogs made?

In open-to-the-public kitchens customers can visit, plus the company's own kitchen in Irvine, California, according to its FAQ. The site also lists a store locator for pickup, but the pages read on 2026-09-27 do not print a pickup price list, so this page prices only what is published online.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/faq · checked 2026-09-27

Do you need a vet's prescription to order?

The pages read on 2026-09-27 print no veterinary sign-up or prescription requirement for these Fresh Frozen recipes - the FAQ instead documents its AAFCO and NRC nutrition standards, and its Vet Portal and Targeted Nutrition lines are listed separately on the site.

Source: https://www.justfoodfordogs.com/pages/faq · checked 2026-09-27
